# Seedance Atlas

This skill is based on the `ZeroLu/awesome-seedance` collection. Use it as a prompt-pattern atlas, not as a rigid template library.

## Core families

### Cinematic film styles
- Best for director-inspired scenes, scale, atmosphere, and filmic motion.
- Emphasize: opening image, subject focus, camera language, lighting, texture, and an escalating final beat.

### Advertising and commercial branding
- Best for product showcases, branded motion, and clean presentation.
- Emphasize: product clarity, material detail, clean background logic, and brand-safe pacing.

### Social media and viral memes
- Best for surprise, absurdity, and fast payoff.
- Emphasize: immediate hook, escalation, reveal, and punchline.

### UGC style
- Best for handheld realism, casual framing, and an everyday-camera feel.
- Emphasize: natural light, small imperfections, believable reactions, and a twist that breaks normality.

### Anime and animation styles
- Best for stylized action, transformation, and consistency-heavy motion.
- Emphasize: character identity, fast cuts, effect continuity, and readable action beats.

### Short-form drama and web series
- Best for emotional outbursts, reversals, and lip-sync heavy scenes.
- Emphasize: close-ups, conflict, emotional turn, and a sharp climax.

### Visual effects and experimental styles
- Best for surreal scale, impossible transformations, and physics-forward spectacle.
- Emphasize: world logic, particle continuity, and a clear before/after shift.

## Prompt skeleton

```text
Style / format / duration
[00:00-00:05] Shot 1 ...
[00:05-00:10] Shot 2 ...
[00:10-00:15] Shot 3 ...
Camera:
Lighting:
Motion:
Audio / dialogue:
```

## Revision checklist

- Make the first 2 seconds readable.
- Keep the main subject visible.
- Name the movement, not just the mood.
- Make transitions explicit.
- End on a clear visual payoff.
